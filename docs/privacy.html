Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Short version: Stride stores all your data on your device only. We collect nothing, share nothing, and track nothing.

What data Stride collects

Stride does not collect any personal information. The app does not require an account, does not ask for your name or email, and does not connect to the internet.

All data you enter — habit names, categories, completion records, and settings — is stored locally on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Third-party analytics and advertising

Stride contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no crash-reporting services that transmit data off your device. No third party receives any information about your use of the app.

Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer any information to outside parties, because we do not collect any information in the first place.

Data deletion

All your Stride data lives on your device. To delete it, simply uninstall the app. There is no account to delete and no server-side data to request removal of.

Children's privacy

Stride does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected, the app is safe for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add features that change how data is handled, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before releasing the new version.
